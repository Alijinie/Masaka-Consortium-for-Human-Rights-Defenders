Know Your Rights • Uganda

What police & communities must respect.

General information only — not legal advice. For urgent cases, always call. Luganda summaries included for accessibility.

🚔

48-hour rule & police bond

A suspect must be produced in court within 48 hours or released on bond. You may apply for police bond; denial must be justified and can be challenged.

Omuzira ateekwa okutwalibwa mu kkooti mu ssaawa 48 oba ayimbulwe ku bondi. (Placeholder Luganda summary.)

🤫

Right to remain silent

You are not obliged to answer incriminating questions or sign statements without counsel. Ask for your lawyer and a witness present.

Oli n'eddembe okusirika okutuusa munnamateeka wo lw'atuuka. (Placeholder Luganda summary.)

What to say at the station →
📰

Documenting & journalism

Peaceful monitoring and reporting are protected civic activity. Seizure of cameras/phones must follow due process — record badge numbers and demand a receipt.

Okukwata ebifaananyi n'okulondoola mu mirembe kikuumibwa. Saba risiti. (Placeholder.)

🌍

Land evictions need orders

No lawful eviction without a valid court order and due notice. Photograph notices, keep land documents safe, and seek an injunction urgently.

Tewali kugoba kwa mateeka awatali ekiragiro kya kkooti. Kuuma ebbaluwa. (Placeholder.)

🛡️

Safety for women defenders

Threats, digital harassment and SGBV retaliation can be reported confidentially. Survivor-centred intake; shelter referrals where available (placeholder list).

Okutiisibwatiisibwa kunoonyezebwa mu kyama. (Placeholder.)

📸

If you can, preserve evidence

  • Names, time, place, badge/vehicle numbers
  • Photos of injuries, notices, seizures (back up off-phone)
  • Send a copy to a trusted contact + to counsel
